The Blueprint

Infrastructure & Planning

Dholera's defining characteristic is its master-planned approach, designing the underlying infrastructure before allocating plots for development.

The Core Pillars

Roads & Mobility

A hierarchical road network designed to segregate heavy industrial traffic from residential areas, facilitating smoother movement.

Underground Utilities

Planning for power, water, and sewage systems to run underground, aiming for reliable service and cleaner aesthetics.

Digital Networks

Provisions for Information and Communication Technology (ICT) infrastructure to support administrative and commercial functions.

Industrial Corridors

Specific zoning for non-polluting industries, supported by planned logistics and transport links.

Sustainable Planning

Incorporating water treatment and green spaces into the broader city design for long-term viability.

Development Creates Opportunity

The transition from planned infrastructure to an active city takes years. Early property buyers typically enter the market during this development phase, anticipating that property values will adjust as the infrastructure becomes operational.

Think Long Term

Large-scale urban development is a gradual process. While the plans for Dholera are extensive, execution occurs in phases. We advise all our clients to approach property purchases here with a long-term holding strategy, rather than expecting short-term speculative gains.
