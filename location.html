Evaluating Plot Locations

Not all land in Dholera is equal. When we recommend a property, we look at its specific positioning within the master plan.

  • Proximity to proposed town planning (TP) roads.
  • Zoning regulations (Residential vs Commercial vs Industrial).
  • Distance from planned central amenities and utility nodes.
  • Accessibility from the main arterial networks.
