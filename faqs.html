General Overview

Dholera Special Investment Region (SIR) is a major greenfield smart city project located in Gujarat, India. It is a key node on the Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) and is being planned to house non-polluting industries, residential zones, and world-class infrastructure.
Dholera is being developed in phases. The Activation Area (Phase 1) is currently undergoing rapid infrastructure development. The entire master plan is a long-term vision expected to span over the next two to three decades.
The Activation Area (approx. 22.5 sq km) is the immediate focus where foundational infrastructure is being built first to kickstart industrial activity. TP (Town Planning) schemes refer to the broader zoning layouts (TP 1, TP 2, etc.) that dictate land use (residential, commercial, green spaces) across the entire 920 sq km SIR region.
