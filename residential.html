Buyer's Guide

What to Consider Before Buying

When evaluating residential plots in Dholera, we help you review several critical factors to ensure the property meets your objectives.

Get Residential Options
We assess the plot's proximity to planned town planning (TP) roads, commercial zones, and public amenities to understand its future context.
We verify that the development has obtained necessary NA (Non-Agricultural) approvals, plan passes, and RERA registration where applicable.
Reviewing the dimensions, FSI (Floor Space Index) allowances, and internal road widths to ensure the plot supports your intended construction.
Checking the developer's timeline for providing basic infrastructure within the project, such as internal paving, water connections, and boundary walls.
