The Process

How It Works

Acquiring property in a developing region requires a structured approach. Here is our step-by-step process designed to ensure clarity and security for buyers.

1

Requirement Gathering

We begin by understanding your investment goals—whether you are looking for residential use, commercial development, or long-term land holding—along with your budget and preferred timeline.

2

Master Plan Review

We consult the Dholera SIR Development Plan to identify Town Planning (TP) schemes and zones that align with your requirements, ensuring the property's designated land use matches your intent.

3

Site Selection & Visit

We shortlist available plots and organize a comprehensive site visit. You will view the physical location and observe the surrounding infrastructure progress firsthand.

4

Due Diligence

Once a plot is selected, we assist in verifying the necessary documentation, including title clearance, NA (Non-Agricultural) approvals, and RERA registration where applicable.

5

Financial Processing

We outline the payment schedule based on the developer's terms and assist with coordinating banking processes, including specific pathways for NRI transactions if required.

6

Registration

Upon completion of payments, we facilitate the drafting of the sale deed and coordinate the formal registration of the property at the sub-registrar's office.

7

Post-Sale Services

We remain available to provide ongoing updates regarding regional infrastructure progress, plot maintenance, and subsequent development phases.

Begin Step 1

Share your requirements with our team and let us start the process of finding your property in Dholera.
